Blog archive hero carousel — manual banners
============================================

Upload these files HERE (folder on disk):

  uploads/blog/banner/

  blog-banner-1.jpg
  blog-banner-2.jpg
  blog-banner-3.jpg
  … (jpg, jpeg, png, webp, or gif)

  /blog/banner/blog-banner-1.jpg
